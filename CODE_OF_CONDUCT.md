# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community. Participants should be constructive, assume good faith, respect different experience levels, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, threats, and publishing private information without consent.

Report incidents privately to the repository maintainers through the security/contact channel configured in the repository. Maintainers may remove content, close issues, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

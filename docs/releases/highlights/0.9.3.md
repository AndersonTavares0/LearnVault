A polish release: more ways to make the study space yours, course categories and a richer Progress screen. Your original files are never moved, renamed or changed.

- **Look and feel:** eight surface palettes (Warm paper, Cool stone, Crisp contrast, Kindle sepia, Forest mist, Nord frost, Rosé pine, Solarized), each in light and dark. New options for card style (bordered, elevated, soft fill), page width (focused, standard, wide), a clean sans-serif font, reduced motion and the default My learning view. Your accent colour now tints navigation, surfaces and borders while keeping 4.5:1 contrast.
- **My learning:** cleaner grid cards, with a menu on hover or focus and progress bars only once a course is started, and a compact two-by-two habit rail. **Course categories** can be assigned from a course card or the course page and filter My learning (stored in your browser).
- **Progress:** the year heatmap has five intensity levels, a legend and tooltips with the date, lessons, focus minutes and whether the day counted. The columns are balanced and the badges are more compact.
- **Settings:** Language sits under Appearance, the weekly goal updates with immediate feedback, and the Focus timer is organised around presets.
- **Lessons and notes:** your notes in the lesson overview read as clean text, and the curriculum borders are clipped cleanly.

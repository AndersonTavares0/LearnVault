Paths that invite

Lesson film   /   04:18
04:18
14:02CC⋯

A path is an invitation, not simply a way through. Notice how the garden’s narrow gravel walk bends just before the old pear tree. That small turn keeps the destination in view, while leaving the arrival a little uncertain.

As you watch, look for the places where movement slows. What’s doing the work: a change in texture, a low branch, a glimpse beyond the wall?
